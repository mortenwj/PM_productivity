# Sharing policy — SSA-L material and prior bid extracts

This bundle may include plain-text extracts under `docs/tender-reference/extracted/`. They are derived from real tender responses (Norwegian SSA-L Bilag 2 / kravtabeller, Danish bilag, Finnish liitteet, etc.) and related working files.

**Distribution owner:** Morten Jensen (Product Management, Public 360°). Recipients are named individuals only.

## Who may receive this bundle

- Tietoevry employees (or contractors under a Tietoevry agreement) who need the **`tender-response`** Cursor skill for **active or planned Nordic public-sector bid work**.
- Not for customers, subcontractors outside our chain, competitors, or public repositories.

## Confidentiality

- Treat all `extracted/` content as **internal confidential** — supplier bid language, implementation detail, and in some files **commercial or pricing context**.
- Do **not** commit `source/` or `extracted/` to a **public** Git remote. In PM_productivity these paths are **gitignored** by design.
- Do **not** post extracts to Teams channels, wikis, or ticket systems unless your line manager agrees the audience is bid-confidential and need-to-know.

## What recipients may do

- Use extracts as **prior art** when drafting new requirement answers (structure, compliance phrasing, cross-references).
- Run the **`tender-response`** skill with **Stage 0a** grep/read against `docs/tender-reference/extracted/` in a local workspace.
- **Verify** every factual claim via `profile:tender-evidence` (wiki, Ratatoskr, ADO, code) before any text goes into a live tender.

## What recipients must not do

- **Forward** this zip, individual `.txt` files, or large paste blocks to others without the distribution owner’s OK.
- **Copy verbatim** paragraphs into a live bid without human review and adaptation to the current RFP (requirement IDs, deployment model, evaluation criteria).
- **Assume** prior answers are still true for product, security, or roadmap — dates and capabilities change.

## Retention

- Keep local copies only while needed for bid work you are involved in.
- Delete the zip and extracted folders when the tender closes or you leave the workstream, unless records management requires otherwise.

## Reporting issues

If you find customer-marked confidential material that should not be in the library, or a recipient no longer needs access, contact the distribution owner so the share list can be updated.
