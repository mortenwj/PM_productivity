## 1. Background — why this cycle is different

### UIM (primary capacity pull)

- **Status (Sep 2026):** **Offer delivered** — awaiting customer decision. **No-bid is not an option**; if we win we deliver the contract, if not we still pursue the same **platform direction** via **CO1/CU1/PP2** (phasing and capacity may shift, not the strategy).
- **27** commitment Features; **~45–75 person-months**; peak **5–8 FTE average (9–14 peak)** if awarded.
- **~70–80%** of risk in **platform spine + Search/FFUI** — **Core UI**, **Core BL**, **Integrations**, **PP (BRE + workflow history)**.
- **433895** customer entities / platform spine (**D1** = phasing + KR scope, not in/out of spine); **MK-77** search (**D2** = contract vs roadmap milestones).
- **Decision Support & Transparency: 0** UIM Features — capacity for **merger + Copenhagen + DK**, not spine.

### Copenhagen discovery (product bets, not a parallel OKR factory)

- **AST / KK / NB:** WCAG, domestic-cloud AI, PE usability, publication/approval, governed AI, metadata/search — **market direction**, not conditional on winning any one engagement.
- **D3 default:** Accelerate **Flow/PE (PP1/PP2)**, **CU2**, **AI2** patterns — not net-new objective per discovery line; **continue regardless of individual bid outcomes**.

### eDoc merger (company objective: *Succeed with eDoc product merger by Q2 2026*)

- Program overlay; team KRs on **CU3, TR2, INT2, DS3, BL2** — not six duplicate “merger OKRs.”

### AI initiatives (separate strategy, one company thread)

- **GoforIT / tenders / KK 435503: B + C + selective A** ([agent appendix](agent-strategy-appendix-d7.md)).
- **CO3** + **AI1/AI2** (Sebastian / AI & Insights); **INT1**, **BL3**, **TP2** as dependencies.
- **Not:** feature-count AI OKRs on every team; merge placeholder objectives *AI infrastructure* and *Support AI adoption* into AI1/AI2.

### AI Software Factory (engineering delivery — not CO3)

- **What it is:** A **governed, repeatable delivery system** (intent → spec → implementation → test → deploy) with human approval gates, shared context, and traceability — not ad hoc laptop AI usage.
- **Why it matters for objectives:** It **enables CO1/CO2** (spine, FFUI, BIF modernisation, desirability/a11y) and can **accelerate CO3** native-agent delivery — but it is **internal product engineering**, not customer-facing “AI adoption.”
- **Company level:** **CO1–CO3 unchanged** — **no fourth company objective** for “engineering efficiency.” Treat the factory like the **eDoc merger**: a **program overlay** with its own KRs and owner, optionally referenced in **CO1** only where output is **customer-visible platform delivery** (not a global efficiency %).
- **Defer:** Company-wide **engineering efficiency %** KRs until **baselines** exist (spec lead time, correction rate, review load, defect escape) — industry and internal planning figures apply to **coding slices only** and are **not** QCLT commitments this cycle.

| Element | Content |
|---------|---------|
