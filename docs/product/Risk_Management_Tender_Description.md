# Risk Management Approach

## Overview

Our risk management framework is a comprehensive, integrated system that ensures proactive identification, assessment, mitigation, and continuous monitoring of risks across all aspects of our operations. We follow Tietoevry's industry-standard risk management process, which is aligned with ISO 9001 requirements and integrated into our development and delivery processes.

## Risk Management Framework

### Integrated Approach

Risk management is embedded throughout our organization and integrated into all key processes:

- **Project Delivery**: Risks are documented in status reports and project risk lists, tracked in status meetings and steering group meetings
- **Product Development**: Risk identification and mitigation plans are integral to sprint planning and product team check-ins
- **Sales Process**: Sales case risks are identified and managed in risk logs and introduced in Decision Gate meetings
- **Business Operations**: Business and privacy risks are documented in our enterprise GRC system (Archer)
- **Continuous Improvement**: Monthly business risk reviews ensure high-impact risks receive leadership attention

### Risk Domains

We systematically manage risks across multiple domains:

1. **Project Risks**: Tracked through project status reports, risk lists, and regular project reviews
2. **Product Development Risks**: Integrated into agile sprint planning with dedicated risk assessment tasks
3. **Sales Risks**: Managed through structured risk logs and decision gate processes
4. **Business Risks**: Documented and monitored at the unit level with quarterly leadership reviews
5. **Privacy Risks**: Mandatory Privacy Risk Assessments (PRA) for all products, aligned with GDPR requirements
6. **Cyber Security Risks**: Comprehensive security risk assessments and threat modeling for all products

## Risk Assessment Process

### Risk Identification

Risks are identified through multiple channels:

- **Proactive Assessment**: During sprint planning, feature design, and project initiation
- **Threat Modeling**: Systematic security threat analysis during development phases
- **Privacy Risk Assessments**: Mandatory assessments for all products before release
- **Continuous Monitoring**: Daily standups, sprint retrospectives, and regular reviews
- **Incident Analysis**: Root cause analysis of issues to identify systemic risks

### Risk Analysis and Evaluation

We use a standardized risk matrix approach to evaluate risks based on:

- **Likelihood**: Probability of the risk occurring (Very Low to Very High)
- **Impact**: Severity of consequences if the risk materializes (Very Low to Very High)
- **Risk Rating**: Combined assessment determines risk level (Low, Medium, High, Very High)

Risks are categorized using a color-coded system:
- **Red Zone**: Requires immediate mitigation actions
- **Yellow Zone**: Requires monitoring or mitigation
- **Green Zone**: Acceptable risk level

### Risk Assessment Activities

Our risk assessment process includes:

1. **Risk Identification**: Systematic identification of potential causes and event sequences
2. **Risk Analysis**: Evaluation of likelihood and impact for each identified risk
3. **Risk Evaluation**: Comparison against acceptance criteria to determine required actions
4. **Documentation**: All risks are documented in appropriate tools (Archer, Azure DevOps, project risk lists)

## Risk Mitigation and Controls

### Risk Treatment

For risks that cannot be accepted, we implement appropriate controls and mitigation actions:

- **Control Implementation**: Security solutions, process improvements, technical safeguards
- **Residual Risk Assessment**: Evaluation of remaining risk after controls are applied
- **Progress Monitoring**: Continuous tracking of mitigation actions and their effectiveness
- **Escalation**: High-priority risks escalated to management and leadership teams

### Security Controls

Our security risk management includes:

- **Threat Modeling**: Systematic analysis of security threats during design and development
- **Application Security Risk Assessment**: Comprehensive evaluation before development begins
- **Static Application Security Testing (SAST)**: Automated daily testing covering OWASP security standards
- **Penetration Testing**: Annual external security assessments by certified security companies
- **Security Architecture Reviews**: Regular evaluation of security controls and architecture

### Privacy Controls

Privacy risk management ensures GDPR compliance:

- **Privacy Risk Assessments (PRA)**: Mandatory assessments for all offerings before release
- **Annual Reviews**: At least annual PRA reviews, plus ad-hoc assessments for significant changes
- **Hierarchical Approach**: Generic and solution-level risk management to avoid duplication
- **Third-Party Assessment**: Evaluation of sub-processors and their privacy controls

## Integration with Development Processes

### Agile Development Integration

Risk management is seamlessly integrated into our agile development methodology:

- **Sprint Planning**: Risk assessment tasks are created and estimated as part of sprint planning
- **Daily Standups**: Risks are raised and discussed in daily team meetings
- **Feature-Level Risks**: Risk assessment required for all new features before implementation
- **User Story Risks**: Risk analysis performed during sprint planning for user stories and bugs
- **Risk Fields**: Dedicated fields in Azure DevOps for risk level and risk description

### Risk Levels in Development

We categorize development risks as:

1. **High Risk**: High probability of issues affecting security, performance, API, or database
2. **Medium Risk**: Limited impact affecting a single module
3. **Low Risk**: No known risk of compromising the solution

### Threat Modeling Process

For security-critical development, we follow a structured threat modeling approach:

1. **Explain & Explore**: Create technical diagrams showing components, data flows, and boundaries
2. **Discover Threats**: Systematic threat identification using STRIDE methodology
3. **Prioritize & Fix**: Prioritized security bugs added to backlog with mitigation plans

## Continuous Monitoring and Review

### Ongoing Risk Management

- **Daily Monitoring**: Risks discussed in daily standups and tracked in sprint boards
- **Sprint Reviews**: Risk status reviewed in sprint retrospectives
- **Monthly Reviews**: Business risk reviews with leadership team for high-impact risks
- **Quarterly Reviews**: Unit-level holistic risk reviews to assess process effectiveness and risk awareness
- **Annual Audits**: External ISAE 3000 Privacy audits and security penetration tests

### Production Monitoring

Post-production risk management includes:

- **Performance Monitoring**: Continuous comparison of performance metrics across releases
- **Automated Security Testing**: Daily SAST scans covering OWASP security standards
- **Incident Management**: SWAT process for critical customer or product issues
- **Major Incident Management**: Structured process for handling significant incidents

## Governance and Accountability

### Roles and Responsibilities

- **Risk Submitter**: All employees can identify and report risks
- **Risk Owner**: Accountable for monitoring and managing assigned risks
- **Mitigation Action Responsible**: Performs mitigation actions within budget and time constraints
- **Leadership Team**: Overall responsibility for unit risk management and escalation handling
- **Risk Manager**: Ensures continuous risk management activities across the organization
- **Privacy Manager**: Main contact for GDPR/privacy matters and privacy governance
- **Security Manager**: Drives security handling and incident response

### Reporting and Escalation

- **Regular Reporting**: Top risks reported monthly to Industry Risk Management forum
- **Escalation Path**: Sub-unit risks → Public 360° Managers → Leadership Team
- **High-Priority Escalation**: High/Very High risks escalated weekly to Leadership Team
- **Status Reports**: Risk status included in project status reports and delivery status reports

## Tools and Documentation

### Risk Management Tools

- **Archer (GRC System)**: Enterprise risk management system for business and privacy risks
- **Azure DevOps**: Risk tracking integrated into work items (Features, User Stories, Bugs)
- **Project Risk Lists**: Standardized templates for project risk documentation
- **Status Reports**: Integrated risk reporting in project status reports

### Standards and Templates

- **Tietoevry Risk Matrix**: Standardized risk assessment template with likelihood and impact definitions
- **Risk Types**: Comprehensive definitions of different risk categories
- **Privacy Risk Assessment Templates**: Structured templates for PRA documentation
- **Threat Modeling Guidelines**: Process documentation and training materials

## Compliance and Certification

Our risk management approach supports:

- **ISO 9001**: Quality management system requirements
- **ISO 27001**: Information security management requirements
- **GDPR**: Privacy risk assessment and compliance requirements
- **ISAE 3000**: External privacy audit requirements
- **Industry Standards**: Alignment with Tietoevry corporate risk management standards

## Continuous Improvement

Risk management is a living process that evolves through:

- **Process Reviews**: Quarterly unit-level reviews of process effectiveness
- **Retrospectives**: Sprint retrospectives identify process improvements
- **Audit Findings**: External audit recommendations drive process enhancements
- **Incident Learning**: Root cause analysis of incidents leads to preventive measures
- **Stakeholder Feedback**: Regular input from teams and management for process refinement

## Conclusion

Our comprehensive risk management framework ensures that risks are proactively identified, systematically assessed, appropriately mitigated, and continuously monitored. By integrating risk management into all aspects of our operations—from product development to project delivery—we maintain a culture of risk awareness and continuous improvement that protects both our organization and our customers.
