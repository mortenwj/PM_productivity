```
=== Value proposition / Selling points ===
*Meet legal and archival requirements—ensure PDF files are PDF/A compliant for hand-over to national archives (e.g. Arkivverket) and long-term preservation.

*Automate quality assurance: the validator runs on a schedule, checks PDFs for PDF/A compliance, and converts non-compliant files so you avoid manual checks and last-minute rework.

*Keep your archive consistent and future-proof with a single, standardized format that remains readable and valid over time.

*Reduce risk of rejected deliveries: compliant documents are marked and non-compliant ones are converted before hand-off, so you avoid delivery failures and re-submissions.

*Scale to large volumes with configurable queries (e.g. by sub-archive, file size, or date) and optional separate conversion server for 24/7 processing.
```

## Bullet summary (for quick review)

1. **Compliance** – Meet legal/archival requirements and PDF/A for hand-over and long-term preservation.  
2. **Automation** – Scheduled validation and conversion; no manual checking or last-minute rework.  
3. **Consistency** – One standard across the archive; future-proof and readable over time.  
4. **Risk reduction** – Compliant docs marked, others converted before hand-off; fewer rejections and re-submissions.  
5. **Scalability** – Configurable queries and optional separate server for high volume and 24/7 processing.
