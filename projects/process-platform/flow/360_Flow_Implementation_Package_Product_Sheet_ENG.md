### Problem

Many public-sector organizations handle cases through fragmented tasks, documents, and manual coordination. Processes live in spreadsheets, local checklists, and tacit knowledge. That makes it harder to deliver consistent service, meet deadlines, demonstrate compliance, and give managers a reliable view of workload. Custom development is costly, slow, and hard to maintain through platform upgrades.

---

### Solution

**360° Flow** brings governed, end-to-end process support into **Public 360°** — process models, phases, checklists, milestones, and ProcessView on the case. Work is structured from initiation to completion without standalone BPM or bespoke development.

This **implementation package** is a fixed-scope delivery engagement: Tieto designs, configures, tests, and go-lives **two production-ready processes** built from **standard shipped building blocks**. You provide domain knowledge and validation; we deliver predictable outcomes and a clear handover to operations.

---

### Key benefits for public sector organizations

- **Predictable delivery** — Fixed-scope package with defined milestones; no open-ended consulting.
- **Faster time to value** — Reuse proven building blocks and delivery patterns instead of starting from scratch.
- **Operational clarity** — End-to-end structure with phases, checklists, and milestones on every case.
- **Compliance-ready foundation** — Progression auditability and role enforcement are part of the Public 360° platform.
- **Included platform capacity** — When you sign this delivery contract, **platform capacity for two active process models is included** at no additional fee under the introductory pilot offer (see below).

---

### Package includes

- Process design, configuration, system test support, UAT support, and go-live for **two processes**
- Project management and configuration documentation (as-built)
- User orientation / handover session for process owners and key users
- **Platform entitlement** for **two active process models** (bundled with the signed delivery contract)

---

### Key features

Structured process models | Case phases & milestones | Checklists | Parallel process activities | ProcessView | Deadline & status visibility | Standard Public 360° integrations (within agreed scope)

### Pilot offer (2026)

Introductory pilot offer for delivery contracts **signed by 31 December 2026** (subject to change): platform capacity for **two active process models** is included at no extra fee **when bundled with this implementation package**. A third process requires separate implementation services and capacity fees. Offer terms are fixed at contract signature.

*Active process model:* a published process model with at least one connected case in the production environment. Full metering terms are set out in the contract appendix.
