## Speaker notes (internal — Sales)

**What changed in pilot vs standard Playbook**

| Playbook (standard) | Flow pilot (now) |
|---------------------|------------------|
| Level 2 — up to 5+ processes without platform fee increase | **2 active process models** included — with signed **delivery contract** only |
| Capacity can be discussed separately from implementation | Capacity and implementation are a **bundle** for the introductory offer |
| Self-service modeling path at Level 3 | **3 enablement sessions** in package; full Level 3 / Enterprise PMC **not** in pilot base |
| Generic "fixed implementation package" | Named SKU: **`FLOW-IMPL-2PROC`** (price from Enablement catalogue) |

**What to say**

> "We are in a controlled pilot. The entry offer is a fixed implementation package for **two end-to-end processes**. When you sign the delivery contract before end of 2026, **platform capacity for those two processes is included** at no extra fee. A third process requires both implementation services and a capacity fee. This is not the final GA packaging — it is a time-limited pilot offer."

**What not to say**

- ❌ "Two free processes on the platform" (without delivery contract)
- ❌ "Up to five processes included" (Playbook GA language)
- ❌ "Unlimited self-service modeling" (pilot — Tieto delivers 2 processes; 3 sessions enable **next** processes on shipped blocks only)
- ❌ "Flow replaces Process Engine" (coexistence; PE not in Flow meter)

**Guardrails (must hold on every deal)**

1. Signed **delivery contract** (`FLOW-IMPL-2PROC`) before promising included capacity  
2. **31 December 2026** deadline for introductory offer on **new** delivery contracts  
3. **Shipped building blocks only** unless Enterprise / add-on sold explicitly  
4. Process 3+ = **implementation + capacity** (see entitlement addendum)  
5. **360° Process Engine** processes are **not** counted in the Flow active-process meter
