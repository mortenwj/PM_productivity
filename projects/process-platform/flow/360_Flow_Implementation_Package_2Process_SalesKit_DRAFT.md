## What this package is

**360° Flow** brings governed, end-to-end process support into Public 360° case handling — process models, phases, checklists, milestones, and ProcessView — without standalone BPM or custom development.

This **implementation package** delivers **two production-ready processes** designed and configured by Tieto, built from **standard shipped building blocks**. The customer validates domain logic and accepts the solution at defined milestones.

**You get (with signed delivery contract):**

- Two operational processes live in production on 360° Flow
- Process design, configuration, testing, and go-live support
- Documentation of delivered configuration
- **Three enablement sessions** (intro, process mapping, checklist hands-on) so your team can configure **next processes** on shipped building blocks
- Handover to operations
- **Platform entitlement** for 2 production processes at no additional capacity fee (limited offer — see Commercial summary)

**You do not get (in base package):**

- Platform entitlement for 2 processes **without** a signed delivery contract for this package

- Enterprise / Level 3 tooling beyond what the three sessions cover
- Custom work units or components outside the shipped catalogue
- Unlimited process changes after handover
- Third process or beyond without expansion SKU

## Active process metering and invoicing

The contract must define how **additional processes** are measured and invoiced. 360° Flow includes **built-in monitoring** of the number of active process models in the customer environment.

### Definition — active process model (metering unit)

An **active process model** counts toward the customer's process entitlement when **both** conditions are met:

| Criterion | Definition |
|-----------|------------|
| **Published** | The process model is published (available for operational use — not draft-only) |
| **In use** | At least **one case** is connected to the process model |

A process model that is published but has **no** connected cases does **not** count as active. A draft model with cases does **not** count until published.

**Authoritative definition:** [`360_Flow_Commercial_Spec_v1.md`](360_Flow_Commercial_Spec_v1.md) §5.1. Edge cases **O1b–O1e** in spec §14 — **must be resolved before first customer contract**.

### Built-in monitoring

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Capability** | Platform monitoring reports the **count of active process models** per customer environment |
| **Purpose** | Entitlement compliance, expansion sales, and invoicing for processes above contracted entitlement |
| **Contract reference** | Customer acknowledges that Tieto uses this monitoring to determine billable process count |

### Entitlement and invoicing logic

| Entitlement | Under limited offer (with signed delivery contract) |
|-------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| **Included** | **2** active process models at no additional platform capacity fee |
| **Above entitlement** | Each additional active process model is **billable** per the contract and Enablement price list |

| Invoicing element | Contract must specify |
|-------------------|----------------------|
| **Included count** | 2 active process models (or post-offer entitlement per GA catalogue) |
| **Unit price above entitlement** | **TBD NOK** per active process model per **TBD** period (month / quarter / year) |
| **SKU** | `FLOW-CAP-PACK-*` and/or Process Engine per-process band — **TBD** Enablement |
| **Measurement basis** | Count of active process models per definition above, as reported by platform monitoring |
| **Billing trigger** | Recurring invoice when active count **exceeds** contracted entitlement in the measurement period |
| **Implementation vs capacity** | **Capacity** (active process fee) is separate from **implementation** (delivery contract for configuring a new process) — both may apply when adding process 3+ |
| **Notice** | **TBD** — e.g. customer notified when active count reaches entitlement limit |

**Sales guardrail:** Adding a third process typically requires **both** (1) implementation/delivery services to configure and go live, and (2) recurring capacity fee for the additional active process model unless covered by a pre-purchased pack.

### E.6.3 Invoicing of additional processes

When the number of **active process models** exceeds the **contracted entitlement**:

1. **Each additional active process model** is invoiced at **TBD NOK** per **TBD** billing period, per Enablement catalogue (`FLOW-CAP-PACK-*` or equivalent).

4. The customer agrees that the **monitoring count** is the authoritative basis for capacity billing, subject to dispute resolution per the master agreement.
5. Billing in arrears based on **monthly peak** active process model count in **production** (locked).
