# Process Framework – Pilot  
**Internal One-Pager**

## 1. Why This Pilot Exists
Public-sector case handling faces a structural challenge:
- Cases must be handled consistently, yet no two cases are identical  
- Organizations need control and compliance, without sacrificing flow and efficiency  
- Traditional task- and document-centric systems do not support true end-to-end processes  

The **Process Framework** in Public 360° addresses this through the principle of **Individual Standard**:  
Standardized structure and automation, with flexibility where real-world variation is required — without bespoke development or upgrade debt.

This pilot is the first concrete step to validate this in practice.

---

## 2. What Is Being Piloted

### Pilot Scope
- Delivery of **one complete end-to-end process**
- Built on the **Process Framework in Public 360°**
- Covering the full lifecycle from initiation to closure
- Delivered as a ready-to-use process

This corresponds to **Single Process Delivery / Enkel prosessleveranse**.

### Standardization as Part of the Pilot
In addition to delivering a working process, the pilot is also used to:
- Identify reusable process structures, rules, and patterns  
- Validate which components are suitable for standardization  
- Feed proven components back into the Process Framework as reusable templates and accelerators  

The goal is to strengthen the platform foundation and enable faster, more predictable delivery in future projects.

---

## 3. How the Pilot Is Delivered

### Delivery Model
- **Tieto owns the delivery**:
  - Process design
  - Configuration in Public 360°
  - Rules, automation, and integrations within scope
- The customer contributes domain knowledge and validates the delivered process

There is no customer self-service process modeling in this pilot.

### What This Tests
- That the Process Framework supports real, regulated end-to-end processes  
- That Tieto can deliver processes faster and more predictably than bespoke work  
- That pilot deliveries can incrementally strengthen the platform through reusable assets  

---

## 4. Who the Pilot Is For
Primary success audience:
- **Business owners / process owners**
- **Tieto consultants**

Other stakeholders (IT, procurement, end users, leadership) are secondary in this phase.

---

## 5. What Value the Pilot Must Demonstrate

### For Business / Process Owners
- Clear end-to-end process structure  
- Built-in rules, deadlines, and compliance  
- Improved predictability and transparency  

### For Tieto (Delivery)
- Reduced bespoke development  
- Repeatable delivery patterns  
- Lower delivery risk and effort  

### For the Product & Platform
- Reusable components validated in real projects  
- Stronger standard templates  
- Reduced lead time for subsequent implementations  

---

## 6. What Success Looks Like
The pilot is successful if:
- A working end-to-end process is delivered using the Process Framework  
- The process delivers real value in a live or pilot context  
- Delivery is faster and safer than bespoke alternatives  
- Consultants see clear reuse and scaling potential  

---

## 7. What Is Explicitly Not in Scope
- Customer self-service process modeling  
- Full Process Framework rollout  
- Hosted / SaaS subscription offering  
- Pricing or procurement validation  
- Extensive customer-specific adaptations that cannot reasonably be reused across customers  

---

## 8. What Comes After the Pilot
If successful, the pilot enables:
- Delivery of additional single processes  
- Expansion toward Hosted Processes  
- Broader Process Framework adoption  

The pilot establishes the foundation for scalable productized delivery.
